ATSFY Technologies has been issued an ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certificate for its Information Security Management System (ISMS). We treat information security as an ongoing organisational responsibility.

ISO/IEC 27001:2022

What this means

The certification relates to ATSFY Technologies' organisational Information Security Management System within the formally defined certificate scope.

This is an organisation-level certification. It does not mean that every ATSFY product, marketplace listing, customer deployment or third-party service has been separately certified.
