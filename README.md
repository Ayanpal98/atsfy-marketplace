# ATSFY Marketplace

### Everything ATSFY Builds. One Marketplace.

**ATSFY Marketplace** is the central digital ecosystem of **ATSFY Technologies**, an AI technology company and venture studio built from Tripura, India.

The marketplace brings together ATSFY's **AI products, SaaS platforms, digital solutions, industry technologies, and emerging ventures** in one discoverable platform.

## 🔎 SEO, AEO & GEO

The Marketplace is structured to improve discoverability across traditional search engines and AI-powered search systems.

The platform focuses on semantic visibility around:

* AI company in Tripura
* AI technology company India
* Technology company in Tripura
* Software company in Agartala
* AI startup India
* SaaS products India
* AI solutions for businesses
* Business automation solutions
* AI venture studio
* CareerTech
* HealthTech
* EdTech
* AgriTech
* HospitalityTech
* FitnessTech
* RetailTech

Rather than keyword stuffing, the platform uses structured content, semantic relationships, product descriptions, internal linking, metadata, FAQs, and structured data to help search engines and AI systems understand the ATSFY ecosystem.

## 💻 Technology

The Marketplace is intentionally lightweight and scalable.

Current implementation:

* HTML5
* CSS3
* Vanilla JavaScript
* Responsive design
* JSON-LD structured data
* SEO metadata
* Open Graph metadata
* Semantic HTML
* Static deployment
