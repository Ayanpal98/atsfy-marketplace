A passing check is not a contract or payment approval. Confirm scope, delivery schedule, GST, third-party charges and payment milestones in a written proposal.

How floors are protected

  • Private floor configuration is stored only in Vercel server environment variables.
  • The evaluator requires a valid Supabase session and app_metadata.role = admin.
  • Missing or invalid floor entries fail closed; the browser cannot approve a price by changing client-side code.
  • Below-floor requests receive scope-reduction guidance, never the confidential floor amount.

The desk remains approval-disabled until ATSFY enters its real delivery costs and management-approved floors.
