Frequently asked questions

A concise guide to ATSFY Marketplace, its software catalogue, digital services, geographic coverage and how to make an enquiry.

About ATSFY Marketplace

What is ATSFY Marketplace?

ATSFY Marketplace is the product and services directory of ATSFY Technologies. It brings together published ATSFY software products, SaaS platforms, venture initiatives, industry solutions and digital services in one place.

Who operates ATSFY Marketplace?

ATSFY Marketplace is operated and published by ATSFY Technologies, an AI technology company and venture studio based in Tripura, India.

Where is ATSFY Technologies based?

ATSFY Technologies is based in Agartala, Tripura, India. The marketplace provides a central place to explore its published products and services.

Products and services

What kinds of software products are listed?

The catalogue covers software and platform initiatives across career and HR technology, fitness, hospitality, healthcare, education, retail, business reporting, agriculture and sustainability, industrial operations, construction and startup support. Browse the product directory.

What digital services can I enquire about?

Published service categories include website development, WhatsApp automation, branding and logo design, social media management, AI chatbots and integration, marketing and local SEO, custom SaaS development, business automation, dashboards, MVP development, startup guidance and education digitalization. Browse all services.

Does a LIVE catalogue label guarantee that a product is available?

No. Catalogue status labels are maintained by ATSFY Marketplace and are not independent guarantees of uptime, public access, adoption, performance or commercial availability. Follow the official product link and confirm current access with ATSFY.

Where can I find current product information?

Use the product directory to find each product's official website. Product-specific access, feature availability and current status should be confirmed on that official product website.

Location and enquiries

Does ATSFY serve businesses outside Tripura?

ATSFY Technologies is based in Tripura, India and presents its products and services to a wider Indian and global audience. Delivery mode, availability and project fit are confirmed during the enquiry and scoping process.

Are the marketplace provider profiles, ratings and project counts verified?

The marketplace network contains demonstration and sample content. Sample provider profiles, ratings, project counts, requirements and activity must not be treated as verified real-world marketplace data.

How can I request a quote or discuss a project?

Email atsfy@atsfy.tech with your business or institution name, the product or service you are interested in, your requirements and preferred contact method. ATSFY can review the scope and confirm the next steps.

Are service prices fixed?

Final pricing depends on the agreed scope, features, integrations, delivery requirements and support needs. Request a project-specific quotation from ATSFY rather than assuming a universal price.

Have a project in mind?

Share what you are trying to build or improve. ATSFY can review the requirements and discuss a suitable next step.
