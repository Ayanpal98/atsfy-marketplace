The assistant runs on its own ATSFY Sales Agent application origin. Its public chat and enquiry features use that application's existing server-side APIs. The private CRM is not embedded here.

What it includes

Public AI assistant

Helps visitors discuss service needs and explore possible ATSFY solutions in English, Hindi and Bengali.

Consent-based enquiries

Visitors choose whether to submit contact information for follow-up. Optional conversation saving is separate from contact consent.

Private sales workspace

Authorized administrators can review leads, maintain activity history, plan follow-ups and prepare proposal drafts.

Human approval boundary

Proposal drafts require administrator review. The assistant does not issue binding quotes or send automated outbound messages.

Frequently asked questions

Is the embedded assistant the same live application?

Yes. The embedded panel loads the public ATSFY Sales Agent application. You can also open it in a separate tab.

Does embedding expose the administrator CRM?

No. The CRM is a separate private workspace with its own administrator sign-in and authorization checks.

Will ATSFY automatically message me?

No automatic outbound message is sent by the public assistant. Any follow-up is handled according to the consent you provide and ATSFY's review process.
