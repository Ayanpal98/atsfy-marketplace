Product overview

A frontend-first workspace prototype for rubber operations. The reviewed interface includes modules for intake and procurement, farmer records, quality, inventory, production, sales and orders, finance, logistics, documents, reports and an AI Copilot. The current source seeds demo records and persists application state in browser local storage.

  • Procurement and intake workflow screens
  • Farmer CRM and quality-lab views
  • Inventory, production and stock-transfer screens
  • Sales orders, invoices and payment views
  • Logistics, documents, reports and AI Copilot interface
